# Collection Brief — Botanica Nocturne

First Propulsa design collection.

## Concept

A dense, engraved-style botanical illustration — olive branch as the primary motif, fig as a secondary motif reserved for a future second drop within the same collection — drawn in the manner of a 19th-century naturalist plate or apothecary label. Used as a border or all-over pattern depending on product.

## Colorway logic

Deep espresso ground with ivory linework; gold reserved for a single accent leaf or vein — never used as a fill or dominant color. Two additional colorways (Inverse, Warm Stone — see `colorway-spec.md`) extend the same logic rather than introducing new color relationships.

## Why it fits the brand

Most editorial of the directions considered. Strongest on hero photography — reads as intentional and collected rather than mass-produced, which matters for justifying premium pricing on a print-on-demand product. Works as a dramatic border on flat goods (towels, table linens) and as a subtler all-over or single-motif print on homeware and apparel, so the same concept scales across product types without feeling diluted.

## Constraints for anything designed under this collection

- One botanical family only (olive, with fig as the sanctioned secondary — no other species without a new collection brief).
- Gold is an accent, never a ground or a fill.
- No rounded/geometric "friendly" rendering of the botanical — keep it engraved/linework, not flat illustrative.
- Every product's artwork should be one of the 3 motif forms in `motif-system.md`, not a new interpretation.
