# Colorway Spec — Botanica Nocturne

Hex values in `brand/palette.json`, matched to live site tokens.

## Signature (Espresso Ground)

- Ground: espresso `#2a211a`
- Linework: ivory `#f5f0e8`
- Accent: gold `#a67c3d`
- Use: the hero colorway. Default for hero photography and lead SKU images.

## Inverse (Ivory Ground)

- Ground: ivory `#f5f0e8`
- Linework: espresso `#2a211a`
- Accent: gold `#a67c3d`
- Use: lighter option for the same range; where a dark ground isn't practical (e.g. warm-climate apparel later) or simply to diversify a variant set.

## Warm Stone

- Ground: stone `#d9cfc1`
- Linework: espresso `#2a211a`
- Accent: gold `#a67c3d`
- Use: a quieter third option — softer contrast than Signature or Inverse.

## Accent finish variants

Where a product needs more color variety than 3 grounds provide (e.g. the towel range's 24 variants), vary the accent metal rather than inventing a new ground:

- Gold `#a67c3d` (standard)
- Gold Light `#c9a15e` (brighter/warmer alternative — site has no separate "bronze" token, use this instead)

## Rules

- Gold/Gold Light is always an accent (single leaf or vein, or the hairline border) — never a fill or a ground.
- Ground + linework pairs above are fixed; don't mix a Signature ground with an Inverse linework, etc.
