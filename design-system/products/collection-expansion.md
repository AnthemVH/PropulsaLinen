## Sequencing note

Kitchen/dining, dining linens, bathroom, and stationery (items 1–20) are grouped ahead of home furnishings and wall art because they reuse the towel's dense-border-on-rectangle logic most directly. Wall art (30–35) is placed before travel/accessories/apparel despite being visually striking, because it's still flat-panel reuse rather than requiring new placement/fit artwork. Apparel (47–50) is last — it needs the most bespoke work (garment placement, sizing per pattern piece).
